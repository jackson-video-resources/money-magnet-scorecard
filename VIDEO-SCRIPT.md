# Your result videos: 30 to 45 seconds each

Each result can have its own short video. Someone who scores low sees a different video from someone who scores high, so each one speaks to exactly where that person is and asks for one thing. Film them on your phone, upright, face on, somewhere quiet. One take is fine.

## The four beats

1. **Name their result (about 5 seconds).** "You scored [their band]. That means [what it means, in one line]."
2. **The one thing (about 10 seconds).** The single reason people at this score are stuck. Not a list.
3. **What changes when they fix it (about 10 seconds).** What their life or business looks like the other side of it.
4. **The next step (about 10 seconds).** "Click the button under this video. It's [what the next step is], and [what they get from it]."

## Example (an interview coach, the middle band)

> "You scored 'getting close'. That means you know what you're doing, but you're not getting offers yet. Here's why: your stories. You're telling them, but they don't prove anything. Fix that, and the same interviews start turning into offers. Click the button under this video. It's my interview workbook, and it walks you through your three best stories, step by step."

## Rules
- One video per result. Say the result's name in the first sentence.
- One ask, and it's the button under the video.
- Talk to one person: "you", never "you guys".
- Keep it under 45 seconds. Shorter beats polished.

## Putting it on your scorecard
Send the video to your computer, then tell Claude or Codex: "This is the video for my [result name] result", and give it the file. It shrinks the file, puts it in the right place and puts your scorecard live again (AGENTS.md, "Result videos").
